## About Me

I am a Research Assistant in the School of Computing at the University of Utah. My research lies at the intersection of eye tracking, visualization, and computational modeling of attention and interactive behavior. I study how gaze and multimodal interaction traces reflect task-driven user behavior, and I develop cognitively grounded models for predicting user scanpaths and evaluating interfaces.

## Research Interests

- **Eye tracking and visualization:** gaze and multimodal interaction traces of task-driven behavior
- **Computational modeling:** cognitively grounded models of attention and scanpath prediction for interface evaluation
